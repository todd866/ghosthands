# GhostHands XIAO RP2040 compact case

Original clean-room enclosure for a bare, headerless Seeed Studio XIAO RP2040.

- Board reference envelope: 21.0 × 17.8 mm (Seeed Studio mechanical data)
- Assembled outside size: approximately 28.0 × 24.8 × 7.2 mm
- Two parts: upright tray and upside-down snap cap with hidden side detents
- Wide USB-C opening, two BOOT/RESET access holes, and an RGB LED window
- Designed for support-free FDM printing with a 0.4 mm nozzle
- Suggested material: matte black PLA, 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill

Upload `ghosthands_xiao_rp2040_case_print_plate.stl` to a print bureau. It
contains both watertight parts already separated and oriented support-free.

The 0.30 mm-per-side cap clearance is intentionally conservative because this
is a first-print design and has not yet been physically fitted to a board. Two
localized 0.15 mm-interference detents provide retention without making the
whole cap tight. If the bureau's printer runs tight, lightly sand the two small
side beads rather than the case body.

The tray's 22.70 mm validated overall width includes those two retention beads.
The normal 21.80 mm tray body fits the 22.40 mm cap cavity with 0.30 mm clearance
per side; at the seated position, the 22.70 mm bead span is relieved by 23.40 mm
pockets. The larger tray bounding box therefore does not describe the mating body.
